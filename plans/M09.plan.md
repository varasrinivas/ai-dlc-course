> Track 2 — The Three Phases (color `var(--t2)` #0d9488) · Audience: **both** · ~30 min
> Key concepts (curriculum map): deployment artifacts, observability, sequential knowledge
> handoff, context preservation for future bolts.
> Continuity: closes Track 2 and bolt 1's story arc (M05 intent → M08 sign-off). The

> speed is manufactured. Phase module → two SVGs.

## Objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. List the deployment artifacts a bolt ships with (build, config, migration steps,
   runbook delta) and place the human sign-off at the exposure boundary.
2. Tie observability to acceptance criteria: every AC that promises behavior gets a
   metric or alert that would notice its absence.
3. Write a bolt-close handoff: what shipped, decisions, deviations, operational
   learnings, open questions for bolt N+1.
4. Explain sequential knowledge handoff — why bolt N+1's Mob Elaboration opens by
   reading bolt N's close-out, and what breaks when it doesn't.

## Hook

Monday of week two, 15:10. UW-1a is signed off, and the temptation is to call bolt 1 done
and grab UW-1b. The ops engineer blocks the doorway: "Done means deployed, observed, and
written down." Forty minutes later staging is running the increment, one alert exists that
would fire if a below-threshold request ever skipped the queue, and a two-page close-out
sits in docs/ — including the one thing nobody predicted (the outreach buffer never once
hit its 50-entry flush). Tuesday's Mob Elaboration for bolt 2 will open by reading that
file. This is the phase teams skip, and it's the phase that makes the next bolt faster
than this one.

## Concept outline

1. **Ship the bolt** (`<h2>`)
   - The AI generates deployment artifacts like any other artifact: build output, config
     delta, migration steps, runbook delta — reviewed, not improvised.
   - Deploy sign-off is the exposure boundary from M02's decision table: the first place
     real PHI could flow; a human decides, every time.
   - Bolt-sized increments make deploys boring — the goal; big-bang releases are the
     old tempo smuggling itself back in.
2. **Observe with intent** (`<h2>`)
   - Observability traces to acceptance criteria, the same way tests do: S2's "queue
     entry ≤ 60s" (M05) becomes an alert that fires when the promise breaks — coverage
     for runtime.
   - Explicit errors (M04's enabler) operationalized: fail loud, log the score
     immutably, alarm on the guardrail (any score < 0.85 without a queue entry).
   - Operational learnings are inputs to the next Inception, not noise: the
     never-fired flush is design feedback.
3. **Preserve context: the sequential knowledge handoff** (`<h2>`)
   - The bolt-close artifact: what shipped, decisions made (with links), deviations and
     why, operational learnings, open questions for bolt N+1 — two pages, written by
     the AI, validated by the humans who lived the bolt.
   - Sequential handoff: bolt N+1's Mob Elaboration opens by reading bolt N's close-out;
     new joiners onboard from the chain, not from tribal memory.
   - The compounding claim, made concrete: skipping the close-out re-litigates decisions
     (cost visible in M06's parked-question discipline); writing it is how 76-day
     outcomes get manufactured bolt by bolt.

## Recap bullets

1. Done means deployed, observed, and written down — a bolt that merges but doesn't
   close is a bolt the next team pays for.
2. Deploy sign-off sits at the exposure boundary; deployment artifacts are generated
   and reviewed like any other artifact.
3. Observability traces to acceptance criteria the way tests do — every promised
   behavior gets a signal that notices its absence.
4. The close-out (five sections, two pages) is the sequential knowledge handoff: bolt
   N+1's Mob Elaboration opens by reading it.
5. Track 2 complete — next → M10 · Adaptive Rigor: not every change deserves the full
   ceremony.
