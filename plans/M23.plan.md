## Hook

Everything so far was scales; this is the recital. The intent on the wall is the one
from M05, now fully funded: "Cut prior-auth turnaround from 4.2 days to under 24 hours
for auto-approvable requests, without a single determination below threshold skipping
nurse review." The team has a dual-steered repo, a decision log five bolts deep, a
rigor policy, gates that drill clean, and an engine policy with receipts. The kickoff
session's job is to turn that intent into four executable bolts — and the first
question the engine asks is one the team correctly refuses to answer on reflex:
"Scaffold NestJS now, or continue the existing TypeScript structure and port later?"
Deferred. Decided in the room. Logged. That's the course, working.

## Concept outline

1. **The capstone contract** (`<h2>`)
   - Four bolts, in dependency order: B1 AuthRequest intake API (extends UW-1a/1b) →
     B2 ClinicalCriteria matching + AUTO_APPROVE_THRESHOLD path → B3 nurse review
     queue → B4 determinations + notifications (consumes the notify epic's UW-N
     slices).
   - Each bolt: one Mob Elaboration, construction sessions per UW, Mob Construction
     per increment, PHI-review stage where the rigor policy triggers it, bolt close.
   - The stack decision (NestJS scaffold vs continue TS+Jest) is consequential and
     deferred to humans at kickoff — either is legitimate; the decision gets a record.
2. **Full Inception, run** (`<h2>`)
   - The kickoff session: plan-gated engine, intent restated, requirements traced to
     the guardrail clauses, tagged questions (most now answered BY the chain — the
     payoff moment: watch how few questions survive contact with decisions.md).
   - The backlog artifact: per bolt — UWs sized by checkpoint, ACs (testable,
     traceable, edge-bearing), gates listed, dependencies named.
   - Parity experiment optional per M22 policy: this epic qualifies (most
     consequential decomposition left).
3. **From backlog to bolt schedule** (`<h2>`)
   - Sequencing logic: B1 first (everything depends on intake's contracts); B2 before
     B3 (the queue routes what scoring scores); B4 last (consumes all three).
   - Known constraints pre-loaded from the chain: eligibility staleness ≤ 24h (I-1),
     E4 semantics (I-2), urgent SLA 4h, no side channels, structured outreach log —
     the capstone inherits five bolts of context memory instead of re-deciding it.
   - Exit artifact: docs/M23-capstone-inception.md + the kickoff transcript — M24
     opens by reading both.
