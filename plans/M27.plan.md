## Hook

The last bolt is a convergence: everything the portal has decided must now become a
record someone can stand behind. A Determination is born on two paths — the machine's
(score ≥ 0.85, components attached) and the nurse's (REVIEWED, with her decision and
identity) — and both paths end in the same immutable shape. The notification slices
built weeks ago as practice — the renderer, the preference lookup, the undeliverable
routing — bolt on against their pinned contracts like they were made for this,
because they were. And the bolt's real exam isn't a feature at all: pick any
determination in staging and walk backwards — request, score, queue history,
decision, message sent. If that walk ever breaks, nothing else this portal does
matters. It doesn't break. That was the point of every artifact since M02.

## Concept outline

1. **What bolt 4 builds** (`<h2>`)
   - The Determination record: immutable, created from AUTO_APPROVED (score +
     components as rationale) or REVIEWED (nurse identity + decision as rationale);
     who/what/when/why always answerable.
   - Notification triggers: determination creation → renderer (UW-N1) → channel
     preference (UW-N2) → delivery; undeliverable → outreach routing (UW-N3) — the
     practice slices integrated against their M18 contracts.
   - The audit trail as a first-class surface: request → score → queue transitions →
     determination → notification, reconstructible from records alone.
2. **Integration bolt discipline** (`<h2>`)
   - Consuming pre-built slices = M18's merge discipline at bolt scale: contracts
     re-verified before integration (NotificationTarget unchanged?), union gates on
     the whole, seam tests added.
   - The double-send edge (M15's [integration] question, now due): the claims system
     also notifies on some determinations — decided: portal suppresses when the
     claims feed covers the event type; tested.
   - PHI through the last mile: the notification renderer's negative test
     (no clinical content) runs against real Determination fixtures now — same test,
     higher stakes.
3. **The audit walk** (`<h2>`)
   - The test that matters: given any determination id, reconstruct full provenance
     from persisted records — no logs required, no tribal memory.
   - Why it works: every bolt paid in: B1's statuses, B2's immutable scores, B3's
     transition records, B4's rationale fields — the chain of custody is the
     system's shape, not a report bolted on.
   - The compliance officer returns (M11's hook): stage definitions + bolt logs +
     the audit walk = an SDLC she can verify in an afternoon. Capstone code
     complete; M28 ships it.
