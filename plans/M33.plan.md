## Objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. Explain why a sprint→bolt switch is a staged migration, not a Monday cutover — the
   three habits (calendar, board, decision cadence) that a memo doesn't change.
2. Remap a Jira/Rally board onto AI-DLC artifacts: epic→bolt, story→unit of work, story
   criteria→M06 criteria, points→bolt lead time; tracker records *what*, chain records
   *why*.
3. Sequence a migration: training-wheels first bolts dual-tracked with the closing
   sprint, first bolt chosen for learning not impact, decider protected (M29), coupled
   units sequenced the M06/M18 way, measured by M31's two numbers not velocity.
4. Name the half-migration failure mode — keeping both operating systems.

## Concept outline (3 sections, ≤300w each)

1. **What you can't flip overnight** — the three sprint habits; inversion-by-decree gives
   the worst of both; migrate in stages with training-wheels first bolts + a coach.
2. **Your Jira or Rally board after the switch** — the remap table in prose; two rules
   (chain owns *why*; migrate the board incrementally).
3. **Sequencing the migration** — first bolt for learning (M10 low-risk), protect the
   decider (M29), coupled units (M06/M18), measure M31's two numbers, ceremonies starve
   by bolt 3; the half-migration failure.

## Lab (dual-path, both engines)

- **Path A — Understand It:** a migration plan for the provider-visibility squad with
  three planted flaws (brownfield first bolt; standup+board kept "for safety";
  velocity-as-green-light; part-time decider). 3 questions + answers.
- **Path B — Build It with AI:** convert a real Jira story ("As a provider I want to see
  why an auth is pending…") into a bolt Inception artifact — restate intent, slice into
  2–4 units of work with testable/traceable criteria, flag a dependency (serialise or
  stub), park unknowns → `docs/M33-migration-inception.md`. Both engines, same structure.
