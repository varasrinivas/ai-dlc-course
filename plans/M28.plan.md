## Objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. Ship the portal in rings: staging → pilot provider groups → full, with a human at
   every exposure boundary and alarms live before traffic.
2. Observe the promise, not just the process: the intent's numbers (turnaround vs
   24h target, zero below-threshold skips) as the primary dashboards.
3. Run incident response with the audit walk as the debugging tool — and turn the
   incident into a recorded learning.
4. Write the program close-out: the capstone chain curated into what shipped, the
   numbers, the decision register, and the next cycle's backlog.

## Hook

Six weeks after the funding email, the portal takes its first real traffic — three
pilot provider groups, chosen because they phone the most. The deploy itself is the
boring anticlockwork the team designed for: rings, sign-offs, alarms armed before
requests flow. What's not boring is Thursday's dashboard: median turnaround for
auto-approvable requests, 3.1 hours — against a 4.2-day baseline and a 24-hour
promise. The queue alarm has fired zero times, which is the number the CMO actually
checks. And Friday brings the first incident — notification retries hammering an
undeliverable address — resolved in forty minutes because the audit walk made the
path obvious, and closed with a learning that becomes next bolt's first line. The
portal isn't finished. It's alive, and the system that built it is now the system
that runs it.

## Concept outline

1. **Ship in rings** (`<h2>`)
   - Rollout rings: staging (weeks of it, via the bolts) → pilot (3 provider groups,
     real PHI, limited blast radius) → full; each ring transition is an exposure
     boundary with a named human sign-off (M02, unchanged at scale).
   - Deployment artifacts per M09 — build, config, migrations, runbook — plus the
     program-level additions: rollback plan per ring, alarm verification BEFORE
     traffic (an unarmed alarm is a decorative one).
   - The M17 gates ride along: threshold config locked in prod exactly as in dev.
2. **Observe the promise** (`<h2>`)
   - Primary dashboards are the intent's own clauses: median turnaround for
     auto-approvable requests (target < 24h; pilot: 3.1h), below-threshold-skip
     alarm (target: zero, forever), urgent SLA compliance (4h).
   - Secondary: score distribution (B2's learning hook — feeds M31's metrics and
     the quarterly criteria review), queue depth per nurse (the SME's workload
     visibility, live).
   - Incident response: the audit walk is the debugger — Friday's retry storm traced
     in minutes; fix + a new test + a learning recorded; the runbook grows a page.
3. **Evolve: the next cycle** (`<h2>`)
   - The portal is never done: the next backlog is already visible — the provider
     visibility UI (prior-auth-web, the Angular practice repo, consuming B1's GET),
     quarterly criteria-set updates (a rigor-classed change process, not a project),
     the E7 flag mystery (F-1 from M12, still open).
   - Program-level context handoff: the whole chain — steering twins, decisions,
     maps, bolt closes, program close-out — IS the onboarding, the audit defense,
     and the reason bolt cycle 2 starts smarter.
   - Hand-forward to Track 7: the numbers, the adoption story, and the governance
     evidence are exactly what M29–M32 operate on.
