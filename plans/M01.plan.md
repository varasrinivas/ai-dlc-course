> Track 1 — The Case for AI-DLC (color `var(--t1)` #b45309) · Audience: **both** · ~25 min
> Key concepts (curriculum map): AI-managed (full autonomy) vs AI-assisted (narrow tasks);

## Objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. Define the AI-managed and AI-assisted anti-patterns and name the failure mode of each.
2. Explain why narrow-task AI assistance plateaus at ~10–15% gains while the lifecycle
   bottleneck stays untouched.
3. Identify which anti-pattern a given team transcript or working agreement exhibits.
4. State the two dimensions AI-DLC replaces them with (AI-powered execution with human
   oversight; dynamic team collaboration) — as a preview, fully taught in M02.

## Hook

The Prior Auth Portal isn't the org's first AI experiment. Last year, a platform team pointed
an autonomous agent at "build a claims triage bot" and let it run; the demo was dazzling, but
compliance found it approved requests no nurse had ever seen, and nobody on the team could
explain the routing logic well enough to fix it. Meanwhile the intake team adopted AI code
completion and measured a real, honest 12% speedup — and their two-quarter backlog is still
two quarters long. Both teams "use AI." Neither is transformed. Before your team starts the
portal rebuild, you need the vocabulary for why both approaches stall — because you will be
pitched both, repeatedly.

## Concept outline

1. **Anti-pattern one: AI-managed** (`<h2>`)
   - Expect AI to build systems autonomously — intent in, system out, no checkpoints.
   - Failure mode: wrong assumptions compound silently; output nobody can review or own;
     in regulated domains (PHI, UM determinations) unreviewable = unshippable.
   - The tell: "the AI will handle it" — accountability has left the room.
2. **Anti-pattern two: AI-assisted** (`<h2>`)
   - AI on narrow tasks only: completion, snippets, test stubs, commit messages.
   - Real but capped gains (~10–15% as reported): typing was never the bottleneck —
     requirements churn, handoffs, and rework are.
   - The tell: AI never sees the intent, only fragments; every gain is local.
3. **What both miss — and the third model** (`<h2>`)
   - Both mis-place the human: absent (AI-managed) or doing all the cognition (AI-assisted).
   - AI-DLC's two dimensions: AI-powered execution with human oversight (AI plans, asks
     clarifying questions, defers critical decisions) + dynamic team collaboration.
   - Analogy block lands after this section? No — after section 2 (see below). One
     forward pointer: M02 teaches the principles, M04 the evidence.

## Recap bullets

1. AI-managed = full autonomy, no checkpoints: assumptions compound, output is unreviewable —
   in UM/PHI domains, unshippable.
2. AI-assisted = narrow tasks: honest ~10–15% gains that never touch the lifecycle bottleneck.
3. The bottleneck is requirements churn, handoffs, and rework — not typing speed.
4. AI-DLC's answer: AI executes and asks across the whole lifecycle; humans decide at
   explicit checkpoints (formalized in M02).
5. Next → M02 · AI-DLC Core Principles.
