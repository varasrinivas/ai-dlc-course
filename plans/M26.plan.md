## Hook

Bolt 3 is where the software meets the people it was built to respect: the nurses who
decide everything the threshold can't. So the Mob Elaboration looks different — the
nurse SME talks, everyone else mostly writes. Push assignments? "No. Nurses claim
work; you don't know whose queue is drowning." What if a claimed item sits — shift
change, emergency? "Back in the pool at timeout, priority preserved." Urgent first?
"Always. The 4-hour clock started at intake, not at claim." By the time the engine
proposes the state machine, half its transitions were dictated by someone who has
worked a review queue at 2 am. That's dynamic team collaboration doing exactly what
M02 promised.

## Concept outline

1. **What bolt 3 builds** (`<h2>`)
   - Queue entry creation from B2's routing flag — within 60 seconds, S2's clause
     now load-bearing; entries carry priority (urgent first — the 4h SLA from M06)
     and the score components (explainability surfaced to the reviewer).
   - Claim-based assignment: a nurse claims; exactly one owner per item; claim
     timeout returns the item to the pool, priority preserved.
   - The SLA alarm (M09's wire): urgent items nearing 4 hours escalate loudly — the
     no-side-channels decision means the alarm IS the escalation path.
2. **State machines you can audit** (`<h2>`)
   - AuthStatus transitions as an explicit table: RECEIVED → SCORED →
     (AUTO_APPROVED | PENDING_REVIEW) → IN_REVIEW → REVIEWED(approve/deny) → B4;
     requeue: IN_REVIEW → PENDING_REVIEW on timeout.
   - Illegal transitions rejected loudly (M04's explicit-errors enabler): no silent
     coercion, every rejection logged with correlation id.
   - S2's invariant enforced twice: the blocking queue write, plus the
     reconciliation sweep (the M05 lab's split, shipped) — no below-threshold
     request is ever neither-queued-nor-determined.
3. **Designing with the SME** (`<h2>`)
   - The queue's semantics are clinical workflow, not engineering preference: the
     SME is the named decider (M06's jurisdiction rule), engineers propose, she
     disposes.
   - Shadowing beats surveys: claim-not-push, timeout requeue, and workload
     visibility all came from how review actually works at 2 am.
   - What the AI contributes: the state machine formalization, the edge enumeration
     (claim during timeout race, urgent reclassification mid-review), the tests —
     the SME validates semantics she couldn't have written as code.
