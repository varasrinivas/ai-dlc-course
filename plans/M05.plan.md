## Objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. State what Inception takes in (a business intent) and puts out (a validated backlog of
   units of work with acceptance criteria).
2. Judge an intent statement: outcome-shaped, measurable, constraint-bearing — not a
   feature list.
3. Trace the decomposition pipeline: intent → AI-drafted requirements → stories → units of
   work, with question-driven elaboration at every step.
4. Classify the AI's clarifying questions (policy, edge case, data, integration) and
   explain why answered questions become recorded constraints.

## Hook

Leadership's funding email lands Friday (the CFO bought the Thursday pitch — including the
honest attribution). It contains one sentence of intent: "Cut prior-auth turnaround from
4.2 days to under 24 hours for auto-approvable requests, without a single determination
below threshold skipping nurse review." Monday morning, that sentence is the entire input
to Inception. By Wednesday it must be a validated backlog of units of work. Nothing about
this transformation is hand-waving: it is a pipeline with defined artifacts at each stage,
and the AI drives every stage while the team answers what only humans can answer.

## Concept outline

1. **Intent is the input artifact** (`<h2>`)
   - What makes intent decomposable: outcome-shaped ("cut turnaround"), measurable (4.2
     days → 24 hours), constraint-bearing (nurse review non-negotiable) — vs a feature
     list ("build a dashboard") which smuggles design into the ask.
   - The AI restates intent before decomposing — shared intent (M02's bridge-table row)
     starts here; a bad restatement caught in 30 seconds saves a bolt of rework.
   - One intent may spawn several epics; the portal intent spawns intake, scoring,
     review-queue, determinations (the M03 slices, now with their origin visible).
2. **The decomposition pipeline** (`<h2>`)
   - Stage by stage: intent → functional + non-functional requirements → user stories
     with acceptance criteria → units of work sized by the M03 checkpoint rule.
   - AI drafts every artifact; each stage ends with human validation (the Mob Elaboration
     moment — ritual mechanics in M06); artifacts land in context memory.
   - Requirements carry the constraints: "score < 0.85 routes to nurse review queue" is a
     requirement traceable to the intent's guardrail clause.
3. **Question-driven elaboration** (`<h2>`)
   - The engine of quality: at every stage the AI attaches questions where the intent is
     silent — four recurring kinds: policy (who may change the threshold), edge case
     (Member eligible but Provider out-of-network), data (where does eligibility truth
     live), integration (does the portal write back to the claims system).
   - Answers are never chat: each becomes a recorded constraint in decisions.md, and
     downstream artifacts must trace to it.
   - Quality tell: an Inception with zero deferred questions means the AI assumed —
     M01's first anti-pattern wearing a process costume.

## Lab outline

**Lab M05 · 25 min · both audiences**

- **Path A — Understand It:** artifact = the intake epic's Inception trace in three
  stages (intent excerpt → 4 requirements incl. one guardrail with an attached policy
  question → 3 stories, one sliced into UW list). Trace steps: find the guardrail
  requirement's origin clause in the intent; classify each of the 3 attached questions
  (policy/edge/data/integration); verify each UW passes the M03 sizing rule. 3 check
  questions + answers.
- **Path B — Build It with AI (both engines):** unit of work — *run Inception on a fresh
  intent*. Intent supplied: "Providers keep calling to ask where their request is — give
  them visibility into AuthRequest status without exposing member PHI." Engine produces
  `docs/M05-inception.md`: restated intent, 4–6 requirements (at least one guardrail),
  ≥3 clarifying questions each tagged [policy]/[edge]/[data]/[integration], and a draft
  UW list with one-line sizing justifications. No code, touch nothing else.
  - Claude Code / Codex CLI variants: identical prompt, `claude` vs `codex`.
  - Verify: file exists, restatement present, guardrail requirement mentions PHI, ≥3
    tagged questions, every UW justified; no code files. Parity: same artifact; question
    sets may differ, tags and structure must not.

## Recap bullets

1. Inception turns one sentence of measurable intent into a validated backlog of units of
   work — via defined artifacts, not vibes: requirements → stories → UWs.
2. Good intent is outcome-shaped, measurable, and constraint-bearing; feature lists
   smuggle design into the ask.
3. The AI drafts every artifact and attaches questions at every joint — policy, edge
   case, data, integration; answers become recorded constraints in context memory.
4. Zero deferred questions is a red flag, not a compliment: it means the AI assumed.
5. Next → M06 · Mob Elaboration in Practice — the ritual that validates all of this, live.
